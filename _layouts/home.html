Who We Are

Indivisible Carson City is a grassroots group of democracy-loving people from Nevada's Capital City region who support each other in civic engagement. We take action to support the principles and practices of democratic government at the local, state, and national levels.

What We Do

Photo: Josh Hobson

Organize & Mobilize

We attend and organize town halls, rallies, and community forums to make our voices heard.

Photo: Kathleen Bent

Register & Vote

We register voters and help our neighbors make their voices heard at the ballot box.

Photo: Josh Hobson

Hold Leaders Accountable

We connect with local, state, and federal representatives to demand they work for the people.

Photo: Josh Hobson

Inform & Educate

Inform the public of the truth regarding the actions of the Trump regime and their harmful impact on America and the world.
