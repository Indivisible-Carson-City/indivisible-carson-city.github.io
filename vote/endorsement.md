After a membership vote, Indivisible Carson City has announced their endorsement of Teresa
Benitez-Thompson in her campaign to represent Nevada's 2nd Congressional District, in what organizers
call a critical race for the district. Other Nevada Indivisible groups across the state have also
endorsed Benitez-Thompson or are supporting that endorsement.

CD2 has been held by Republicans since it was created following the 1980 census, but local Indivisible
leaders say Benitez-Thompson, a lifelong northern Nevadan, has the experience and community roots to
change that.

> "Benitez-Thompson's work as a social worker, mom, community leader, and Majority Leader of the Nevada
> State Assembly embodies the values Indivisible groups are committed to holding our elected officials
> accountable to: equality, justice, compassion, inclusion, and the protection of our democracy. We
> believe she will carry those values to Washington."
>
> — Kelly Bennett Unger, Indivisible Carson City Leader

A centrist Democrat, Benitez-Thompson has lived in northern Nevada since she was four years old, in
contrast to her opponent, David Flippo, who relocated to Reno only months ago to run for Congress.
Colleagues from across the political spectrum have praised her work ethic and integrity. Assembly
Member Selena Torres-Fossett (D-Las Vegas) has called her one of the most talented lawmakers she has
worked with, while former State Senator Pete Goicoechea of Eureka County, a staunch conservative, has
described her as a good friend, according to the *Nevada Independent* (May 17, 2026).

> "Teresa has a unique perspective having come from a background of social work and roots that run deep
> in Northern Nevada. She has the experience we need and the desire to fight for the best opportunities
> for hard working Nevadans."
>
> — Cassandra Kuretich, Humboldt Indivisible Leader

> "Teresa's experience in the State Assembly and her roots in Northern Nevada prove her qualifications.
> Her opponent is not from Northern Nevada, and his loyalty lies with Trump and not with Nevadans.
> Teresa is the best choice for CD2!"
>
> — Farrell Vaughn, Indivisible Northern Nevada Leader

Endorsing Indivisible groups say Benitez-Thompson's priorities align closely with the concerns of CD2
voters, including affordability, jobs and the economy, protection of public lands, election security,
and healthcare access.

## Endorsing the campaign

- Indivisible Carson City — Carson City, NV
- Incline Indivisible — Incline Village, NV
- Humboldt Indivisible — Winnemucca, NV
- Indivisible Northern Nevada — Reno, NV
- Fallon Indivisible — Fallon, NV
- Northeastern Nevada Indivisible — Elko, NV

## Supporting the endorsement

- Citizens Action Group Indivisible — Mesquite, NV
- Indivisible Las Vegas — Las Vegas, NV

Organizers noted that they have requested that CD2 be designated a target race by national Indivisible
leadership, which would bring additional resources such as phone banking and get-out-the-vote support
to the campaign.
